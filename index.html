          03 — Features

          Built for how work actually piles up

          Rank by importance, not by whatever arrived last

          Every task carries a priority from Low to Urgent, an estimate in minutes, and an optional due time. Sort by any of them, and Margin adds up how much planned work is still open so you know whether today is realistic.

          04 — How it works

          Three habits, five minutes a day

          1. Capture

            Write down everything the moment it comes up. A title is enough — add priority, a date, and an estimate when you know them.

          2. Rank

            Sort by priority and be honest about what’s Urgent. If more than three things are, none of them are.

          3. Place

            Drag tasks onto the calendar. Check the week view on Monday and the day view each morning, then mark things in progress as you start them.

          05 — FAQ

          Questions

          Still stuck? Press ? to see every keyboard shortcut.

          Where are my tasks saved?

          In this browser’s local storage, on this device only. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data will remove them, so use Export to keep a copy.

          How do I change a task’s status?

          Use the status menu on each task to move it between To do, In progress, and Done, tick the circle to mark it done, or drag the card between columns in the board layout.

          What do the priority levels mean?

          Urgent is for things with consequences today. High matters this week. Medium is the default. Low is for nice-to-haves. The bars next to each task show the level at a glance.

          Can I move my tasks to another computer?

          Yes. Choose Export in the footer to copy your tasks, then choose Import on the other browser and paste them in.

          Does it work on my phone?

          The layout adapts to small screens. Dragging is desktop-only, but everything it does — rescheduling and changing status — can also be done from the edit window.
